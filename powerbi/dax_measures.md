# RetailPulse: DAX Measures Library & Calculation Guide

This document contains all production DAX analytical formulas designed for **RetailPulse**. All measures are organized within a dedicated measure table (`_Measures`) in Power BI Desktop.

---

## 1. Core Financial & Volume KPI Measures

### 1.1 `[Net Revenue]`
- **Business Description**: The total net commercial revenue generated by the business after deducting all customer returns and cancellations.
- **DAX Formula**:
  ```dax
  Net Revenue = 
  SUM(fact_sales[line_total])
  ```
- **Format**: Currency (`£#,##0.00`)
- **Baseline Benchmark**: **£9,748,131.07**
- **Explanation**: In our cleaned fact table, sales carry positive `line_total` values and returns carry negative values. Summing `line_total` gives the exact net commercial revenue.

---

### 1.2 `[Gross Sales]`
- **Business Description**: Total top-line sales value generated before subtracting customer returns or refunds.
- **DAX Formula**:
  ```dax
  Gross Sales = 
  CALCULATE(
      SUM(fact_sales[line_total]),
      fact_sales[is_return] = 0
  )
  ```
- **Format**: Currency (`£#,##0.00`)
- **Baseline Benchmark**: **£10,642,110.80**
- **Explanation**: Uses `CALCULATE` to evaluate the sum of `line_total` strictly where `is_return = 0` (positive sales transactions).

---

### 1.3 `[Return Value]`
- **Business Description**: Total monetary value of customer refunds, cancellations, and product credits.
- **DAX Formula**:
  ```dax
  Return Value = 
  CALCULATE(
      SUM(fact_sales[line_total]),
      fact_sales[is_return] = 1
  )
  ```
- **Format**: Currency (`£#,##0.00`)
- **Baseline Benchmark**: **-£893,979.73**
- **Explanation**: Filters for `is_return = 1` to isolate the total refund stream. Returns naturally carry a negative sign.

---

### 1.4 `[Total Orders]`
- **Business Description**: Total distinct invoice count across all transactions (including returns).
- **DAX Formula**:
  ```dax
  Total Orders = 
  DISTINCTCOUNT(fact_sales[invoice_no])
  ```
- **Format**: Whole Number (`#,##0`)
- **Baseline Benchmark**: **23,796 orders**
- **Explanation**: Counts distinct order IDs. In an e-commerce basket, multiple rows share the same `invoice_no`.

---

### 1.5 `[Sales Orders]`
- **Business Description**: Total distinct completed purchase orders (excluding cancellation slips).
- **DAX Formula**:
  ```dax
  Sales Orders = 
  CALCULATE(
      DISTINCTCOUNT(fact_sales[invoice_no]),
      fact_sales[is_return] = 0
  )
  ```
- **Format**: Whole Number (`#,##0`)
- **Baseline Benchmark**: **20,136 orders**

---

### 1.6 `[Total Units Sold]`
- **Business Description**: Cumulative physical units of merchandise purchased by customers.
- **DAX Formula**:
  ```dax
  Total Units Sold = 
  CALCULATE(
      SUM(fact_sales[quantity]),
      fact_sales[is_return] = 0
  )
  ```
- **Format**: Whole Number (`#,##0`)
- **Baseline Benchmark**: **5,572,404 units**

---

### 1.7 `[Average Order Value (AOV)]`
- **Business Description**: The average monetary gross expenditure per completed purchase order.
- **DAX Formula**:
  ```dax
  Average Order Value (AOV) = 
  DIVIDE(
      [Gross Sales],
      [Sales Orders],
      0
  )
  ```
- **Format**: Currency (`£#,##0.00`)
- **Baseline Benchmark**: **£528.51**
- **Explanation**: Uses `DIVIDE` with alternate result `0` to safely handle division by zero.

---

### 1.8 `[Average Items per Order]`
- **Business Description**: Average physical quantity of units per purchase basket.
- **DAX Formula**:
  ```dax
  Average Items per Order = 
  DIVIDE(
      [Total Units Sold],
      [Sales Orders],
      0
  )
  ```
- **Format**: Decimal (`#,##0.0`)
- **Baseline Benchmark**: **276.7 units/order**

---

## 2. Returns & Operational Risk Measures

### 2.1 `[Return Rate %]`
- **Business Description**: The proportion of gross sales lost to returns and cancellations.
- **DAX Formula**:
  ```dax
  Return Rate % = 
  DIVIDE(
      ABS([Return Value]),
      [Gross Sales],
      0
  )
  ```
- **Format**: Percentage (`0.00%`)
- **Baseline Benchmark**: **8.40%**
- **Explanation**: Takes the absolute value of the refund stream divided by gross sales.

---

### 2.2 `[Return Order Count]`
- **Business Description**: Total distinct cancellation and return invoices logged.
- **DAX Formula**:
  ```dax
  Return Order Count = 
  CALCULATE(
      DISTINCTCOUNT(fact_sales[invoice_no]),
      fact_sales[is_return] = 1
  )
  ```
- **Format**: Whole Number (`#,##0`)
- **Baseline Benchmark**: **3,660 return invoices**

---

## 3. Time Intelligence Measures

Time intelligence measures require the active relationship with `dim_date[order_date]`.

### 3.1 `[Previous Month Net Sales]`
- **Business Description**: Net revenue in the prior calendar month.
- **DAX Formula**:
  ```dax
  Previous Month Net Sales = 
  CALCULATE(
      [Net Revenue],
      PREVIOUSMONTH(dim_date[order_date])
  )
  ```
- **Format**: Currency (`£#,##0.00`)

---

### 3.2 `[Month-over-Month (MoM) Growth £]`
- **Business Description**: The net monetary change in revenue compared to the preceding month.
- **DAX Formula**:
  ```dax
  Month-over-Month (MoM) Growth £ = 
  VAR CurrentMonthSales = [Net Revenue]
  VAR PriorMonthSales = [Previous Month Net Sales]
  RETURN
      IF(
          ISBLANK(PriorMonthSales),
          BLANK(),
          CurrentMonthSales - PriorMonthSales
      )
  ```
- **Format**: Currency (`£#,##0.00`)

---

### 3.3 `[Month-over-Month (MoM) Growth %]`
- **Business Description**: Percentage rate of sales growth compared to the preceding month.
- **DAX Formula**:
  ```dax
  Month-over-Month (MoM) Growth % = 
  DIVIDE(
      [Month-over-Month (MoM) Growth £],
      [Previous Month Net Sales],
      0
  )
  ```
- **Format**: Percentage (`+0.0%;-0.0%;0.0%`)

---

### 3.4 `[Previous Year Net Sales]`
- **Business Description**: Net revenue generated in the exact same date range of the previous calendar year.
- **DAX Formula**:
  ```dax
  Previous Year Net Sales = 
  CALCULATE(
      [Net Revenue],
      SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR(dim_date[order_date])
  )
  ```
- **Format**: Currency (`£#,##0.00`)

---

### 3.5 `[Cumulative Net Sales YTD]`
- **Business Description**: Running total of net sales from the beginning of the calendar year to the current date.
- **DAX Formula**:
  ```dax
  Cumulative Net Sales YTD = 
  TOTALYTD(
      [Net Revenue],
      dim_date[order_date]
  )
  ```
- **Format**: Currency (`£#,##0.00`)

---

## 4. Customer Intelligence Measures

### 4.1 `[Total Customers]`
- **Business Description**: Total unique customer entities (including the guest checkout cohort).
- **DAX Formula**:
  ```dax
  Total Customers = 
  DISTINCTCOUNT(fact_sales[customer_id])
  ```
- **Format**: Whole Number (`#,##0`)
- **Baseline Benchmark**: **4,372 customer entities**

---

### 4.2 `[Registered Customers]`
- **Business Description**: Total distinct registered customer accounts (excluding anonymous guest checkouts).
- **DAX Formula**:
  ```dax
  Registered Customers = 
  CALCULATE(
      DISTINCTCOUNT(fact_sales[customer_id]),
      fact_sales[customer_id] <> -1
  )
  ```
- **Format**: Whole Number (`#,##0`)
- **Baseline Benchmark**: **4,371 accounts**

---

### 4.3 `[Guest Checkout Orders]`
- **Business Description**: Total distinct purchase invoices completed by anonymous guest shoppers.
- **DAX Formula**:
  ```dax
  Guest Checkout Orders = 
  CALCULATE(
      DISTINCTCOUNT(fact_sales[invoice_no]),
      fact_sales[customer_id] = -1
  )
  ```
- **Format**: Whole Number (`#,##0`)
- **Baseline Benchmark**: **1,610 orders**

---

### 4.4 `[Guest Revenue Contribution %]`
- **Business Description**: The share of total company net revenue generated by unauthenticated guest buyers.
- **DAX Formula**:
  ```dax
  Guest Revenue Contribution % = 
  VAR GuestRev = CALCULATE([Net Revenue], fact_sales[customer_id] = -1)
  RETURN
      DIVIDE(GuestRev, [Net Revenue], 0)
  ```
- **Format**: Percentage (`0.00%`)
- **Baseline Benchmark**: **15.08%**

---

### 4.5 `[Repeat Customers]`
- **Business Description**: The count of registered customer accounts who have placed more than 1 distinct order.
- **DAX Formula**:
  ```dax
  Repeat Customers = 
  CALCULATE(
      COUNTROWS(
          FILTER(
              dim_customer,
              dim_customer[customer_id] <> -1 && dim_customer[total_orders] > 1
          )
      )
  )
  ```
- **Format**: Whole Number (`#,##0`)
- **Baseline Benchmark**: **3,059 repeat buyers**

---

### 4.6 `[Repeat Customer Rate %]`
- **Business Description**: The percentage of registered accounts who are repeat buyers.
- **DAX Formula**:
  ```dax
  Repeat Customer Rate % = 
  DIVIDE(
      [Repeat Customers],
      [Registered Customers],
      0
  )
  ```
- **Format**: Percentage (`0.00%`)
- **Baseline Benchmark**: **69.98%**
- **Explanation**: 3,059 repeat customers divided by 4,371 registered customers equals 69.98%.

---

### 4.7 `[Average Spend per Registered Customer]`
- **Business Description**: The average lifetime spend per registered customer account.
- **DAX Formula**:
  ```dax
  Average Spend per Registered Customer = 
  DIVIDE(
      CALCULATE([Net Revenue], fact_sales[customer_id] <> -1),
      [Registered Customers],
      0
  )
  ```
- **Format**: Currency (`£#,##0.00`)
- **Baseline Benchmark**: **£1,893.96**

---

## 5. Governance: What We DO NOT Calculate

- **Product Cost, Profit, and Margin %**:
  - The UCI Online Retail dataset **does not provide product acquisition cost, shipping cost, or gross margin**.
  - As senior data analysts, we **never fabricate or assume cost figures**. All calculations strictly measure **Gross Revenue, Returns, and Net Commercial Revenue**.
